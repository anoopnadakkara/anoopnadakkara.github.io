About

Architect. Engineer. Problem Solver.

With over 15 years of hands-on experience in the Microsoft technology stack, I specialise in designing and delivering scalable SaaS applications and cloud-native microservices architectures that solve real business problems at enterprise scale.

My career has taken me from writing first production code on ERP and CRM systems, through leading platform modernisations that replaced decade-old WCF/Silverlight monoliths with cloud-native microservices, to architecting compliance-grade archival systems and high-throughput document processing pipelines.
